## Escalation Rules

- P0: pause invites immediately.
- P1: do not invite more families until fixed or explicitly downgraded with reason.
- P2: fix during current pilot iteration or give a documented workaround.
- P3/P4: queue for hardening or V1 review.

## Safety Review Rule

Safety feedback creates a review queue item. It does not automatically notify other family members, accuse anyone, or trigger AI judgment.

## Data Request Rule

Data request handling must be factual and auditable. Do not promise instant deletion when records may need review, sealing, or audit retention.

## External Pilot Blocker

External pilot invitations remain blocked until this file has real owner names and contact paths.
