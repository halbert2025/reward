## Naming Rules

- `scene_`: full-scene image, usually usable as background/state illustration.
- `barista_`: main cat barista state.
- `guest_`: guest cat state.
- `overlay_`: transparent or semi-transparent atmosphere layer.
- `foreground_`: foreground/counter layer.
- `props_`: reusable small objects.
- `drink_`: completion drink asset.
